# Stage 1 curriculum policy — 新手森林

Stage 1 must behave as a real beginner curriculum, not merely an ID range.

- Ordinary new-word selection starts with lv1 words.
- lv2 words are introduced progressively after the learner has established a beginner base.
- lv3/lv4 words inside IDs 1–300 are not ordinary random new words in Stage 1.
- Due review of an already-seen Stage 1 word keeps priority.
- Distractor meanings come from the active Stage 1 teaching pool and prefer similar level.
- Sentence Quest must use the same active teaching pool.
- Stage unlock requirements must not exceed the effective teachable pool.
- Leaving a learning activity via navigation must not record a wrong answer.

This policy is intentionally independent of the original word-list ordering.
